# CRISP

## What it is:
Useful when you want concise, direct prompts with strong task control.

## CRISP means:
Context - The background or situation
Role - The persona or expertise to act as
Instructions - The exact task or steps to follow
Style - The tone, format, or structure
Parameters - The length, output type, or constraints

## Template:
Context : What is the background?
Role : Who should the model act as?
Instructions : What exactly should be done?
Style : Tone, format, or structure
Parameters : Length, output type, constraints

## Example:
Context : I teach automation testing
Role : Act as a senior playwright mentor
Instructions : Explain page objects
Style : Use simple bullet and beginner-friendly language
Parameters : 6 bullets, short, no jargon

Best for: direct execution, faster prompting, and clean outputs.
