# STAR

## What it is:
Useful when you want structured answers, interview-style reasoning, or step-by-step response framing.

## STAR means:
Situation - The context or background
Task - The goal or what needs to be done
Action - The specific steps or approach
Result - The expected final outcome or format

## Template:
Situation : What is happening?
Task : What needs to be done?
Action : What should the model do?
Result : What should the final output look like?

## Example:
Situation : I am a QA Engineer preparing for interview
Task : I want to explain API testing clearly
Action : Give me a simple answer with one example
Result : A 5-bullet response in easy English

Best for: structured answers and role-based scenarios.
